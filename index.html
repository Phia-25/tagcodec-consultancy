ABOUT US

Helping cooperatives grow with confidence.

The Consultancy Department of the Tagalog Cooperative Development Center provides professional services and technical assistance to cooperatives. Our work focuses on sound accounting, reliable financial information, effective internal controls, compliance, and informed decision-making.

We also share practical accounting and finance resources to make cooperative financial concepts easier to understand.
